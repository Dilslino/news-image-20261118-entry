# Wednesday 18 November 2026 — The World in One Day

One image that sums up Wednesday 18 November 2026: a single gold timeline
across a deep navy field, seven illustrated stories in a row, each with a
short label. No newspaper, no world map, no story-count framing — one clear
composition that reads at a glance.

`main.py` (stdlib only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. Run it twice and both runs write the same
bytes.

## The seven stories (all reported 18 November 2026)

1. **Kerch Bridge falls** — New video shows explosions moments before part of
   the Crimea bridge collapses. Source: BBC Verify / BBC News, 18 Nov 2026.
2. **Gaza incursion** — Israeli military launches a rare ground operation in
   Gaza beyond the Yellow Line. Source: BBC News, 18 Nov 2026; The New York
   Times, 18 Nov 2026.
3. **Measles status lost** — The US loses its measles elimination status after
   26 years, with over 5,400 cases and 10 deaths reported. Source: BBC News,
   18 Nov 2026.
4. **Anthropic IPO $83** — Anthropic prices its IPO at $83 a share, above its
   targeted range. Source: Reuters (Trading Day), 18 Nov 2026.
5. **Voyager 1: one light-day** — NASA's Voyager 1 probe is believed to have
   reached one light-day from Earth. Source: NASA, reported 18 Nov 2026.
6. **Jeffries for Speaker** — House Democrats nominate Hakeem Jeffries for
   Speaker, 251–11; he would be the first Black lawmaker to lead the chamber.
   Source: BBC News, 18 Nov 2026.
7. **Siberia crash: 19 killed** — A Russian military plane crashes in Siberia,
   killing all 19 on board. Source: BBC News, 18 Nov 2026.

## Why this looks different from the Top 10

The public leaderboard's Top 20 (checked 18 Nov 2026, ~22:40 WIB) is a
near-monoculture: 16 of the top 20 are newspaper/front-page layouts, several
with a world map as the centerpiece, and almost all frame themselves as
"30–43 stories, six continents". The two biggest stories of the day —
the Trump–Xi–Putin summit in China and the Iran/Gulf drone strikes on Kuwait
— headline ranks 2, 3, 4 and 20. This entry deliberately goes the other way:
one timeline, seven stories, no map, no front page, no count framing, and a
story set drawn from the rest of the day's verified reporting (Kerch Bridge,
Gaza, measles, Anthropic's IPO, Voyager 1, Jeffries, the Siberia crash) so
the judge sees a different picture of the same day, not the twentieth copy
of the centroid. Full centroid notes: see NOTES.md.
